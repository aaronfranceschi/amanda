Petaluma House in Print
Towards the end of last year, we had the pleasure of renovating Petaluma House for lifestyle blogger, Bellamumma. Working in conjunction with our friends at Custom Construction and *Nina Maya Interiors*, we managed to create a beautiful and inviting home for Nikki and her family. The project itself was a fun one for us here at Plateau Electrical (despite a few 14 hour days). There was a lot of opportunity to flex our creative muscles with unique pendant lighting, LED strips behind mirrors and under cupboards as well as innovative sensor placement to allow for seamless and effective lighting throughout the house.

In terms of practicality, we installed powerpoints with built-in USB outlets throughout the house to allow for charging on the fly. In addition to this we installed 2 wifi extenders and multiple data points throughout the house to make the most of the *new NBN connection.*

We're super proud of how this project turned out and Petaluma House has since gone on to be featured in the April edition of *Elle Magazine!*
