Dear friends and partners,

I’m proud to share a great piece of news with you today! I have been selected by REMODELING Magazine to join the REMODELING Big50, a select group of remodeling business owners who are shining examples of leadership and work ethic in the field. This distinction highlights a year filled with accomplishments for our company and is a welcome validation for our innovative business model.

As you know, dRemodeling is the leading remodeling business in Philadelphia, serving center city and the surrounding areas for 8 years, and counting. During this time, we have become the highest rated bathroom remodeler in the greater Philadelphia region, with hundreds of renovations under our belt and multiple awards won. The company has grown by leaps and bounds, and is now a million-dollar-plus company which will only continue to grow and improve with the recognition gained from the Big50 award.

The Big50 Class of 2016 features owners of remodeling companies that have set exceptionally high standards for professionalism and integrity through exemplary business practices, craftsmanship, and have impact in their community or the industry at large. Big50 remodelers run successful, growing companies of various sizes that have taken the lead in raising industry standards.

This win, being recognized among the 50 best remodeling companies in the country and to be among only 79 Pennsylvania remodelers that have been awarded the distinction since 1986, is humbling and also incentivizing for us. It confirms that the way we’ve chosen to do business and to develop dRemodeling is indeed the right approach moving forward. As REMODELING magazine points out, the Big50 winners “embody some of the most innovative strategies to survive – and flourish – in a changing market.”

I attribute much of the success of my company to our amazing team of designers, technicians, and project managers, as I mentionin this interview for REMODELING Magazine. They get the job done with professionalism while respecting clients’ wishes and deadlines. Their exceptional focus ensures that no detail, however small, falls through the cracks. They enforce my belief that dRemodeling is well on its way to becoming the go-to remodeling business in Philadelphia.
