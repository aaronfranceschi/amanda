WELCOME TO SENLIS
The Senlis collection is inspired by the charming fleur-filled town of Senlis, situated in the northern region of France. While meandering along the Nonette river on the town’s cobblestone streets, through the magnificent Jardin du Roy and visiting nearby Chantilly - where the namesake lace was created - our collection was born..
