Alex Šepkus Inc. was founded by Alex Šepkus, and his friend and managing partner Jeffrey Feero in 1992. Since then, Alex Šepkus jewelry has been known for its distinct design, signature textures and microscopic details. Collected and coveted around the world, Alex Šepkus jewelry is recognized not only for its inimitable design, but for the creative direction in its use of gemstones and unique color combinations overseen by the COO, Candace Edelman.

Made in NYC, no detail of Alex Šepkus jewelry is compromised. From hand finishing, to setting stones, making clasps and hammering textures, master jewelers at the Alex Šepkus studio use microscopes to execute the level of detail expected from these timeless designs.
