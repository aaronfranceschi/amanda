Learn How CCPA Will Impact Your Business
This special web event will be the first in a series of webinars to help educate and prepare businesses for the CCPA. The author of the CCPA, Alastair Mactaggart, and a panel of leading privacy experts will provide insight into:
what you will learn
1. What the key provisions of the law mean to you and your customers:
"Right to Know"
"Right to Say No"
"Right to Have Data Kept Safe"
2. How existing and forthcoming technologies make it possible to achieve the intent of the CCPA
3. How to build a data privacy culture within your enterprise
ABOUT THE SPEAKERS
Alastair Mactaggart,
Chairman, Californians for Consumer Privacy
Alastair believes that all people should have the fundamental right of data privacy and has led the effort to craft the California Consumer Protection Act and promote its adoption.
Justin Antonipillai
Privacy Expert & CEO, WireWheel
Justin is one of the leading experts on privacy and data protection. Prior to WireWheel, he led important international dialogues as Acting U.S. Under Secretary of Commerce for Economic Affairs on privacy.
John Ackerly
Security Expert & CEO, Virtru
John is proud to be helping thousands of organizations take control of the information they store and share online. A longtime privacy advocate, John was previously responsible for technology policy at the White House.
Jocelyn Aqua
Principal, Regulatory Privacy & Cybersecurity, PwC
