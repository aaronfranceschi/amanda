A New York City Home Care Agency
Age & The City Home Care is a leading New York City-based agency that provides unique non-medical dementia care services for people with all types of dementia, from early onset to the severe stages.

Our mission is to help people suffering from Alzheimer’s Disease and other types of dementia to retain as high quality of life as possible through different stages of the disease by staying active at their own home and in their community.
